# 🍰 Food by giamat

**A culinary expansion for Minecraft that turns survival into a kitchen.** Grow new crops, forage wild fruit, set up your own food-processing chain, and cook **80+ new dishes** — from fresh pita and schnitzel to pizza, sushi, gummy candy, and magical cakes. If you've ever wished Minecraft's food system had more depth, variety, and reward, this mod is for you.

> Built for **Minecraft 26.1.2** (Fabric). Requires **Fabric API** and **Java 21**.

---

## ✨ What does this mod add?

### 🌾 Grow & forage your own ingredients
Stock your pantry straight from the world:
- **Crops:** corn and rice you can plant and farm.
- **Wild bushes:** tomato and grape bushes scattered across the plains, plus chili pepper bushes out in the desert.
- **Orchard trees:** banana, lemon, pomegranate, and olive trees that grow harvestable fruit.

### 🔪 A real cooking chain
Ingredients don't just appear — you build them up step by step:
- **Wheat → flour → dough → bread**, with kneading and baking stages.
- Press **olives into olive oil**, mix it with milk into **cream**, then churn **butter**.
- Strain a water cauldron with a **strainer** to collect **salt**.
- Make **tomato paste**, **cheese**, **breadcrumbs**, **gelatin**, **melted chocolate**, and **sweet dough** as building blocks for bigger recipes.

### 🍕 80+ new foods to cook and eat
A huge menu of dishes, each with its own recipe and nutrition:
- **Savory:** pita, schnitzel, sausage, sausage-in-a-bun, hamburger, chicken nuggets (plain & breaded), pizza (with customizable toppings!), sushi.
- **Sweet:** cookies, donuts, challah, popcorn, shoko (chocolate milk), gummy candies in 4 flavors, and a rainbow of fruit **popsicles**.
- **Drinks:** wine, pressed from grapes (delicious, but it'll leave you dizzy 🍷).

### 🪄 Special & magical foods
Some dishes do more than fill your belly:
- 🌶️ **Chili pepper** — grants Fire Resistance.
- 🧈 **Buttered bread** — Regeneration (add salt for an even bigger boost).
- ✨ **Glow berry popsicle** — Night Vision.
- 🟪 **End cake** — eat a slice to teleport.
- ☠️ **Cursed cake** — bake it with a sniffer or dragon egg and it poisons whoever takes a bite.
- 🍲 **Sus cake & sus pizza** — infuse a dish with any Suspicious Stew effect.

### 🧪 Combine anything
Drop **two or more foods** into the crafting grid to create a **combined meal** that stacks every food's effects into one item. Pile on too many and the mix turns your stomach (nausea over 6 foods) — experiment to find the perfect plate.

### ⚠️ Cook it right
Cooking matters. Eating **unbaked** dough, schnitzel, sausage, or nuggets raw will poison and sicken you — and leaving bread in the furnace too long leaves you with **burnt bread** that actually hurts to eat.

### 🧑‍🌾 Villager trades
- **Clerics** buy wheat for emeralds and sell **challah** and **wine**.
- **Wandering traders** stock challah and wine too.

## 🚀 Why download it?

- **Depth without tedium** — meaningful multi-step recipes that feel rewarding, not grindy.
- **Tons of variety** — over 80 items spanning crops, ingredients, meals, sweets, and drinks.
- **Real gameplay impact** — effect-granting foods, farmable trees and bushes, and trade integration.
- **Fits right in** — items slot into the vanilla Food & Drink, Ingredients, and Tools tabs, with familiar Minecraft mechanics.

## 📦 Installation

1. Install [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/) for Minecraft **26.1.2**.
2. Download the latest [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api).
3. Drop both the Fabric API jar and this mod's jar into your `mods` folder.
4. Launch the game and start cooking! 🍳
